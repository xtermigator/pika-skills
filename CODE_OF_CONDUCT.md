# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Inclusive Game Day is an accessibility-centered, non-commercial public-interest project. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and barrier-free environment for everyone — regardless of ability, disability (visible or invisible), sensory needs, neurodivergence, age, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, religion, or level of technical experience.

Everyone who contributes to, uses, or participates in this project is expected to uphold these values.

## Our Standards

**We encourage:**

- Using clear, plain, and inclusive language
- Being patient and kind, especially with people who communicate or process information differently
- Respecting that disability and access needs are often invisible — assume good faith
- Welcoming questions and feedback from people with lived experience of disability
- Acknowledging and crediting contributions generously
- Offering constructive, specific, and actionable feedback

**We do not tolerate:**

- Discriminatory, ableist, or dehumanizing language or behavior
- Dismissing or minimizing someone's access needs or lived experience
- Harassment, intimidation, or unwanted contact of any kind
- Publishing someone's personal information without their consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment

## Accessibility Expectations

Because this project explicitly serves people with invisible disabilities and sensory needs, we hold ourselves to a higher standard of accessibility awareness:

- Provide text alternatives for any images or media you contribute
- Use descriptive link text rather than bare URLs
- Avoid using color alone to convey meaning in documentation or UI contributions
- Write issue titles and PR descriptions that are clear and self-contained
- Be mindful that cognitive load, processing time, and communication styles vary — allow space for that

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open a private issue or contact the project maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion and care.

We are committed to protecting reporters from retaliation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channels — as well as in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, with additional accessibility-specific language reflecting the mission of Inclusive Game Day.
